Google Ads integration

Research with
the source in view.

With the account owner's OAuth permission and the required Google API access, Flow Atelier can list accessible Google Ads customer accounts and request historical Google Search keyword metrics.

These signals support topic and keyword research for digital products. Google Search volumes and advertising competition are identified as Google data; they are not Etsy search volumes or Etsy sales estimates.

The current Google integration contains account-listing and keyword-research operations. It does not create or publish advertising campaigns. API access remains subject to Google's approvals and quotas.
